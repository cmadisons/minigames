🏃 Gap Runner
You run along a line full of holes. Left click to jump, click again in mid-air to double jump, and hold ↓ to crouch under the pipes. Reach the checkpoint flag to clear the level — every level runs faster and throws something new at you.

Click (or Space) to jump · click again while airborne to double jump — your runner flips · hold ↓ (or right-click) to crouch under the pipes, which also dives you back down faster in mid-air. The two dots up top are the jumps you have left. P pauses.

What each level adds

1 · plain holes, gentle pace  ·  2 · wide holes that need the double jump  ·  3 · 🔺 spikes planted on the line  ·  4 · 🚧 pipes down from the sky — hold ↓ to duck under  ·  5 · 🪨 stepping stones over holes too big to cross  ·  6 · 🟠 crumbling ground that drops the moment you land  ·  7+ · all of it, faster and tighter every level.
