Whack-a-Hedgehog

Classic: 🐲 dragon (+3) · 🦔 hedgehog (+3s) · blue Sonic (+5s/+5pts) · 💣 bomb (don't hit!) · 🗝️ keys give no points — you collect them (some drop as ×3 triples!) · 👁️ bosses every 50 pts, in order 1→8: solo eyes, then 5-at-once swarms, a 💣 −30s bomb-eye, an all-in-a-row gauntlet, and a final all-together JACKPOT (rewards scale: key → rare → gold → diamond → 5 of every key!).
Key tiers: 🗝️ normal → 🔑 rare (after you earn 25 keys) → 🔑 gold (spend 25 rare) → 🔑 diamond (spend 25 gold) → 💚 THE Emerald Key (trade 25 diamond → WIN!). Each opens its own chest: rare = 50 all-useful · gold = 25 · diamond = 9 (incl. the 🏠 House: +$100 per whack!). The Emerald Key grants a 🏰 mansion, 💚 emerald hammer, 👑 throne, a daily $1000 + present, and eternal bragging rights. You can only buy/trade UP to the next key tier after fully clearing that tier's chest grid (normal = both grids) — finishing a grid opens the Store & unlocks the trade. ⚡ speed to 50.
Daily (10×10): catch Sonics, avoid hedgehogs — hit 3 hedgehogs & you're out. Most Sonics (60s) or Speedrun 10 (fastest, 3 hedgehogs = fail).
Versus (unlock from a chest): P1 = mouse, P2 = WASD + Space. 🦔=+1, blue Sonic=+2, 💣=−1, 🔑 keys score here (normal +2, rare +4, gold +6, diamond +8; triples ×3). Most points in 60s wins.
