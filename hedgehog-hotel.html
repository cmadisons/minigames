🦔 Hedgehog Hotel

Picky hedgehogs are checking in. Give each one the perfect room!

How to play

  1. 1Hedgehog guests wait at the front desk. Their speech bubble shows what they want.
  2. 2Drag a guest onto a room that matches (or tap the guest, then tap a room). Rooms glow green when they are perfect!
  3. 3Hurry! If a guest waits too long, they curl into a spiky ball and leave. 😤
  4. 4When a room shows a bubble 🛎️, tap the room and pick the right thing from the tray.
  5. 5Happy guests pay coins 🪙 and stars ⭐. Spend coins in the shop after each day. A season is 7 days — get the most stars!
