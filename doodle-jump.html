🦘 Doodle Jump
You bounce on your own — all you do is steer. Climb as high as you can and don't fall off the bottom.
← → steer · Space shoot · you wrap around the edges

← → or A/D to steer, Space or ↑ to shoot. Green platforms are solid · blue ones slide · brown ones crumble · pale ones vanish once you use them. Grab a spring for a big bounce or a propeller to fly. Land on a monster's head or shoot it — anything else kills you.
