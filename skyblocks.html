📜 Goals

    Mine: tap any block and it goes into your bag. Build: pick a block from the bag, then tap empty sky next to something solid.
    💧 Water and 🔥 lava can be picked back up if you put them in the wrong spot. Leave one empty square between them and it fills with cobblestone — over and over, forever. That's how you get unlimited blocks.
