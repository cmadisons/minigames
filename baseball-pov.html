Baseball's POV ⚾

You're not the batter. You're the BALL! You just got smacked — fly over the wall for a home run!

How to play

  • 👆 Phone: put your finger on the game and slide it like a joystick.
  • ⌨️ Computer: arrow keys (or W A S D). Up = fly higher, down = dive lower.
  • 🧤 Don't touch a glove — that's an OUT!
  • ⚡ Your energy runs down. Grab 💨 wind boosts and 🌀 extra spin to keep flying.
  • 🧱 Be higher than the wall when you get there = HOME RUN!
  • 🎯 Bonus: land in the kid's glove in the stands, or splash in the water!
  • 🏏 Tip: tap (or press Space) right when the pitch reaches the bat for more power.

Slide on the game or use the arrow keys. 9 at-bats per game.
