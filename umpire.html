⚾ Umpire Simulator

You're the home-plate umpire. Watch every pitch and make the call!

How to play

  • Watch the ball fly in from the pitcher to the catcher's mitt.
  • Was it inside the invisible strike zone (over the plate, between the batter's knees and chest)?
  • Yes? Press STRIKE (or the S key). No? Press BALL (or the B key).
  • If the batter swings, you don't have to call anything. Just watch.
  • Pitches right on the edge are worth extra points.
  • Two bad calls in one inning and the manager comes out to yell. Listen (L) or eject him (E), but only eject him if your calls have been good!
  • Three wrong calls in a row and you're FIRED!
