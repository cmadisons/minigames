🧮 2048
Slide the tiles. Two of the same number join into one twice as big.

← ↑ → ↓ or WASD to slide · swipe on a touch screen. Every tile shifts as far as it can go, and equal tiles that collide merge. One new tile appears each move — 90% a 2, 10% a 4. You win at 2048, but you can keep going for a bigger tile.
