⬡ Hexagon Fall
A hexagon is balanced on a tower of bricks. Tap a brick to take it out — everything above it drops. Clear your target without letting the hexagon reach the ground.

Tap or click any brick to remove it. Whatever was resting on it falls, and so does the hexagon. It rolls off slopes, so watch the edges — if it touches the ground, or rolls off the side of the tower, the game is over. P pauses.
